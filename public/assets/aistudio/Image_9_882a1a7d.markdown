## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the ethos of an elite educational consultant powered by advanced computational intelligence. Designed for ambitious students, parents, and international applicants navigating high-stakes higher education decisions, the system balances institutional credibility with bleeding-edge technology. The visual atmosphere is calm, authoritative, and deeply perceptive—demystifying complex admission statistics into clear, actionable paths.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern Precision** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- Structured slate canvases paired with deep navy surfaces establish gravitas and stability.
- Translucent, frosted structural panels and subtle glowing gradients signal AI-driven computational depth.
- Tactile, high-legibility cards and interactive nodes create confidence and rapid comprehension on compact touch devices.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast deep values against luminous, crisp backgrounds to maintain effortless legibility and focus.

- **Primary Canvas & Ink (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Deep Navy anchors key navigation anchors, primary text, dark action modes, and brand headers, conveying scholastic prestige.
- **AI Accents & Interactions (`#6366F1`, `#8B5CF6`, `#A855F7`):** Electric Indigo and Soft Violet represent the active machine learning engine, used for match indicators, primary CTA gradients, interactive states, and search highlights.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#334155`):** Crisp slate layers provide distinct surface separations without visual fatigue. `#F8FAFC` serves as the primary canvas ground, with `#FFFFFF` reserved for elevated card surfaces.
- **Match Status Semantic Accents:**
  - **High Match / Verified (`#10B981` on `#ECFDF5`):** Clean Emerald conveys safety, high compatibility, and official institution verification.
  - **Reach / Ambitious (`#F59E0B` on `#FFFBEB`):** Warm Amber denotes stretch goals, selective profiles, and cautionary admission thresholds.
  - **Target / Balanced (`#6366F1` on `#EEF2FF`):** Soft Violet/Indigo badges denote balanced programmatic matches.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp geometric construction, modern proportions, and distinct open counters that optimize small-screen mobile legibility.

- **Headlines:** Use tightened negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`600` to `700`) to assert institutional confidence.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Use tabular or aligned glyphs when rendering match percentages, acceptance rates, and tuition figures to preserve scan lines.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Displayed in uppercase or deliberate medium-to-bold weights with slight tracking expansion to maintain clarity against saturated pill backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for hand-held performance, strict touch targets, and rhythmic vertical scan tracks:

- **Mobile Canvas (Default):** Fluid layout relying on a 4-column structure with `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins and `16px` gutters. Safe areas for top camera cutouts and bottom iOS home indicators are strictly enforced via structural inner wrappers.
- **Tablet / Responsive Scale (768px+):** Scales up to an 8-column layout with `24px` margins and gutters, constraining maximum readable content width to `640px` for discovery feeds and modal views.
- **Component Padding Scale:**
  - `space-xs` (4px): Fine inner tag offsets and icon-to-label gaps.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Form input gaps, pill tag padding, stack item micro-separations.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding and interactive row separation.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Card group separation and hero header vertical padding.
  - `space-xl` (32px): Primary section demarcations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via translucent layers, soft atmospheric drop shadows, and subtle micro-borders:

- **Surface Base:** Ground color is `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Institutional Tiles):** Background `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards, Filters, Swipers):** Background `#FFFFFF` with shadow: `0px 12px 28px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Overlays):** Uses translucent glassmorphism. Background `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `16px` and a bottom divider line in `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Bottom Navigation):** Translucent frosted glass. Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)` backed by a backdrop filter blur of `20px`, enclosed with a subtle top border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` and ambient lift: `0 -8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric curve hierarchy that aligns soft approachability with academic authority:

- **Cards & Sheets:** Base radius is `16px` (`rounded-lg`), providing an organic framing for campus photos, metric grids, and profile comparisons.
- **Modals & Drawers:** Base radius is `24px` (`rounded-xl`) at top edges to emphasize gesture-friendly swipe surfaces.
- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Rendered as full pills (`9999px`) or `10px` rounded rectangles for crisp contrast against larger card bodies.
- **Progress Rings:** Rounded stroke caps on all circular match visualizers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid or dual-tone gradient fill (`#6366F1` to `#8B5CF6`) with `#FFFFFF` text. Height is 48px to satisfy accessible mobile touch boundaries. Subtle glow state on active touch (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1px` border of `#E2E8F0` and `#0F172A` text. Pressing down transitions background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Icon Only:** 44x44px round square with 12px radius or circular pill, `#F1F5F9` surface with `#334155` iconography.

### Cards
- **University Match Card:** High-contrast white card (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px corner radius and 16px internal padding. Features a split header: university insignia and location on the left, interactive Match Score Progress Ring on the right. Metadata row presents Acceptance Rate, Average SAT/GPA, and Annual Tuition formatted as structured key-value chips.
- **Comparison Drawer Card:** Compact horizontal preview card with 12px radius, dual thumbnail imagery, and side-by-side metric comparison differentials.

### Match Score Progress Ring
- Radial SVG progress visualizer (diameter: 44px to 56px).
- Track background stroke is `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`.
- Active stroke: Emerald (`#10B981`) for $\ge 85\%$ compatibility, Indigo-Violet gradient (`#6366F1` to `#8B5CF6`) for $70\text{--}84\%$, and Amber (`#F59E0B`) for reach bands ($< 70\%$).
- Center typography: Bold `13px` match integer with `%` symbol in `label-sm`.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **High Match / Verified:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), `4px 10px` padding. Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, paired with an Emerald shield or checkmark icon.
- **Reach / Ambitious:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, paired with an upward trend chevron icon.
- **Filter Chip:** Height 32px, pill radius. Inactive: `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text. Active: `#0F172A` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Search Header:** 48px height, rounded-full shape or 12px radius, background `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2E8F0`. Prefix icon in `#64748B`, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Includes an AI sparkle quick-prompt icon in `#6366F1`.
- **Filter Selectors:** Bottom sheet selectors with radio rows, supporting check marks in `#6366F1` and bold selected item text.

### Navigation
- **Floating Bottom Bar:** Docked 12px above bottom device margin, height 64px, rounded-full pill container or continuous 20px top-radius frosted sheet (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with backdrop-filter blur). Features 4 to 5 key destinations: Discover (AI Match), Search/Explore, Shortlist, Insights, Profile.
- **Active Navigation Indicator:** Subtle Indigo micro-dot or filled tint pill under the active icon to maintain calm spatial balance.
